Download Entire YouTube Playlists as a ZIP File

Learn how to batch-download full YouTube playlists — every track is processed server-side and packaged into a single ZIP for easy download.

Downloading a single video is easy. But what about an entire playlist — a study soundtrack, a workout mix, or a podcast series? MediaDL's playlist mode lets you grab every item in one go and delivers them as a neatly packaged ZIP file.

How Playlist Downloads Work

When you submit a YouTube playlist URL, MediaDL processes each video in the list sequentially on the server. Once all tracks are converted to your chosen format, they are compressed into a single ZIP archive and made available for download. You don't have to click "Download" for each file individually.

Step-by-Step: Download a YouTube Playlist

  1. Find your playlist on YouTube. Navigate to the playlist page and copy the URL from the address bar. It will contain list= in the URL — e.g. youtube.com/playlist?list=PL...
  2. Paste the playlist URL into MediaDL's input field on the home page.
  3. Choose your format — MP3 for audio-only, MP4 for video. Select a quality level from the dropdown.
  4. Click "Download". A progress bar will show how many tracks have been processed.
  5. Download your ZIP once all tracks are complete. The ZIP file will be named after the playlist.

Tip: Large playlists (50+ videos) can take several minutes to process. Keep the browser tab open while the server works. The download link remains available for 10 minutes after completion.

Supported Playlist Formats

  • YouTube playlists (public and unlisted)
  • YouTube channel "Uploads" playlists
  • YouTube Music playlists (via their public URL)

Are There Any Limits?

For performance and fair-use reasons, playlist downloads are currently capped at 50 tracks per batch. If your playlist is longer, you can split it or download in multiple sessions.

What's Inside the ZIP?

Each file in the ZIP is named using the original video title. Audio files are in MP3 format; video files are in MP4 (H.264). Filenames are sanitised to remove characters that are invalid on major operating systems.
