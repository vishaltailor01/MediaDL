Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 8, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how MediaDL ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our service at mediadl.app.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to operate the service:

  • URLs you submit: Video or playlist URLs you paste into the converter. These are used solely to process your download request and are not stored beyond the duration of your session.
  • Server logs: Standard web server access logs (IP address, browser type, timestamp) retained for up to 30 days for security and debugging purposes.
  • Local storage: Your recent download history is stored in your own browser's localStorage and never sent to our servers.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require you to create an account or provide personal information.
  • We do not use advertising trackers, third-party analytics scripts, or behavioural tracking cookies.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Downloaded Files

Files processed and downloaded through MediaDL are stored temporarily on our servers. All files are automatically deleted within 24 hours of creation. We do not access, review, or share the content of your downloaded files.

4. Cookies

MediaDL does not set tracking cookies. We use browser localStorage (a standard client-side storage mechanism) to remember your theme preference and recent download history. This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to us.

5. Third-Party Services

MediaDL is self-hosted with no embedded third-party analytics or advertising. We load fonts from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com); please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for information on how their CDN processes requests.

6. Children's Privacy

MediaDL is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

8. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact page.
