Copyright Claims

Last updated: April 8, 2026

MediaDL respects the intellectual property rights of others. If you believe that content processed through our Service infringes your copyright, you may submit a claim using the process described below.

Important: MediaDL does not host, store, or distribute third-party content. We provide a conversion tool that processes publicly accessible URLs at the user's request. Filing a false or misleading claim may expose you to legal liability.

How to Submit a Copyright Claim

To submit a valid DMCA-style takedown notice, your claim must include the following information:

  1. Your full legal name and contact information (email address and, optionally, a phone number).
  2. A description of the copyrighted work you believe has been infringed.
  3. The specific URL(s) or content that you claim is infringing.
  4. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the use of the material is not authorised by the copyright owner, its agent, or law.
  5. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information in your notice is accurate and that you are the copyright owner or authorised to act on their behalf.
  6. Your electronic or physical signature.

What Happens After You Submit

Once we receive a valid claim, we will:

  • Review the notice to confirm it meets the requirements above.
  • Take appropriate action, which may include blocking access to the reported URL(s) in our service.
  • Notify you of the outcome within a reasonable timeframe.

Incomplete or invalid notices will not be actioned and may be disregarded without response.

Counter-Notice Process

If you believe your content was removed as a result of a mistaken or misidentified claim, you may submit a counter-notice. Your counter-notice must include:

  • Your full name and contact information.
  • Identification of the content that was removed and its previous location.
  • A statement under penalty of perjury that you have a good-faith belief the content was removed by mistake or misidentification.
  • Your consent to the jurisdiction of relevant courts.
  • Your electronic or physical signature.

Repeat Infringer Policy

MediaDL maintains a policy of terminating access for users who are found to be repeat copyright infringers, in appropriate circumstances and at our sole discretion.

Submit Your Claim

To submit a copyright claim or counter-notice, please email us at copyright@mediadl.example and include all required information listed above. We will not be able to process incomplete submissions.

Contact for Copyright Matters

Please use the subject line "Copyright Claim" or "Counter-Notice" in your email so we can route your message appropriately.
